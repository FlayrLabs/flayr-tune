## Download

| Your computer | File | Plug-in formats |
|---|---|---|
| Mac (Apple silicon or Intel) | `FlayrTune-<version>-macOS.dmg` | AU + VST3 |
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `FlayrTune-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe` | VST3 |
| Linux (x64) | `FlayrTune-<version>-Linux-x64.tar.gz` | VST3 + LV2 |

**Mac:** open the DMG and drag each plug-in onto the folder next to it.
**Windows:** run the installer. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet: click **More info** then **Run anyway**.
**Linux:** extract and run `./install.sh`.

Then restart your DAW or rescan plug-ins. Step-by-step guides for each DAW are in the [README](https://github.com/FlayrLabs/flayr-tune#install).

To verify your download, compare it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` below. See [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/FlayrLabs/flayr-tune/blob/main/SECURITY.md) for signature and provenance checks.
